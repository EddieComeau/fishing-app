# FishDex App Store Positioning

## App Name
FishDex

## Selected Subtitle
Real-Time Fishing Decisions

## Positioning Statement
FishDex is a fishing decision system that helps you choose what to do before you cast.

## One-Sentence Value
Fish smarter with real-time guidance, not guesswork.

## Category
FishDex is a decision and guidance tool.

It is not:

- a logging app
- a social fishing app
- a fish-identification app

## Differentiator
Most fishing apps record what already happened.

FishDex helps you decide what to do next.

## Keywords
fishing, fishing app, fishing guide, fish tracking, fishing conditions, fishing forecast, bass fishing, saltwater fishing, fishing log, angler tools

## App Description

### Hook
Stop guessing what to use.

FishDex helps you make better fishing decisions before you cast.

### What It Does
Get real-time guidance based on:

- conditions
- species behavior
- rig selection
- strategy

### How It Works
Follow a simple flow:

`Start Fishing -> Conditions -> Species -> Rig -> Strategy -> Catch -> Review`

### Key Features
- Smart rig recommendations
- Clear explanations with why and why not
- Real-time fishing adjustments
- Catch tracking and review
- Session-based fishing insights

### Differentiator
FishDex does not just log your catches.

It helps you decide what to do next.

### Close
Fish with more confidence and less guesswork.
